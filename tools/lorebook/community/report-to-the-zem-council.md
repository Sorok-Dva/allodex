Esteemed members of the Council!

In my report I wish to inform the Council of the latest events on the allod of Jigran, of the current policy of the so-called "ruler of Sarnaut", and of my own conclusions and forecasts.

First of all, the esteemed Council needs to understand what the allod of Jigran is. I suspect that not everyone knows its true history, but even those who do will, I think, not mind recalling how it all began.

As is now known, after the Cataclysm a Kanian garrison remained on this allod, holding the fortresses of Dar Ulgorn in the centre of the allod and Dar Leanos in the north. In the south lay the city of Sardrok, near which lived a surviving tribe of the June. Among these June a man was born who would later become known as the Great Mage Firez.

The details of his life are hidden from history, but we know that at some point he managed to become a powerful necromancer, made a pact with Nezeb and then brought the Xadaganians to Jigran. The Great Mage cut Jigran off from the rest of the world with the spell of the Seal of Mystery. A long war began between the Kanian garrison in the north and the Xadaganians in the south, while the June were enslaved by Firez.

The necromancers' spells did irreparable damage to the allod: the vegetation died and the beasts died out. With his sorcery Firez raised all the dead and, turning them into zombies, put them into his service. Meanwhile the dying land could no longer grow new harvests, and the people of Kania could barely hold back the onslaught of the necromancer's armies.

Everything changed around the year 800. One of the June, a youth named Kir-a-Chit-La, managed to escape and reached the allod of Suslanger. There, with the help of a girl named Kel, he made a journey similar to that of Ath-Zak, travelling through other lands. In the end he was able to return to Jigran, killed Firez and freed his June brethren.

However, the allod and its nature had been irreparably blighted by the necromancers' spells. The people had no choice but to use the portal and leave. The Kanians went to Lightwood, the surviving Xadaganians to Suslanger, and all trace of the June was lost...

For 200 years the allod vanished. Then, in our era, it was found: for some time the elves of House de Delice kept the discovery secret, but a few years ago, at the Great Ball, the decision was made to hand it over to the vampires and other undead of the army created that year. For this purpose the ruins of the fortress of Dar Ulgorn were rebuilt as a human city in the style of the Old Era.

Everything changed again with the arrival of Hirkalla, who decided to make this allod her capital. I suppose her decision was influenced by the need to keep the undead armies gathered by Prince Dakkar under control, and there is no better place for that in Sarnaut today than Jigran, steeped as it is in dark power. Hirkalla announced that she would resurrect all the dead and, with the help of the Great Mages, set about carrying out her intention.

Gradually the city filled with the ghosts of those who had once lived. Most of them were among the Kanians and Xadaganians who inhabited Sarnaut at the time the world split into allods, or a little earlier, for in that era the world had its largest population. Now they call themselves the "Awakened".

After a while some of the ghosts began to turn into the living. This was the effect of the spells used by Hirkalla and the Great Mages subordinate to her. The Council knows that such magic exists, and this method of resurrection is one of the secrets of the Conclave. The trouble with it is that it is extremely costly: a single such ritual is equivalent to several months of the XAES's output. That is why the "universal resurrection" is progressing very slowly, and many of the ghosts are unhappy about it.

This is not the only problem facing our new ruler. The elves had already erected on Jigran the Cathedral of Faith of the Church of Light. Before Hirkalla arrived on the allod, it had been occupied by the followers of a certain Hiero. The "shield of faith" they created proved impenetrable to the Darkness and thus became a protection for all who dispute her power. Hiero has proclaimed a "holy crusade" against the goddess of Darkness, and it seems that many are ready to support him. For her part, Hirkalla generously rewards her revived followers, who gradually swell the ranks of her guard and her army.

However, the situation of those who have not been resurrected remains dire. To remain in the state of a ghost and sustain its ethereal body, a ghost needs the energy of a Spark. The only way for a ghost to replenish this energy is to obtain it from a donor. As a result, trade in Spark energy has flourished on a massive scale on Jigran. I note that the Sparks are captured with the Sparkton Packs we invented.

Besides this, there is yet another misfortune: ancient creatures that had slept on Jigran since the time of Firez, awakened by Hirkalla's magic. They hunt Sparks, which are their natural food. More and more dark creatures are drawing towards the city. To protect the inhabitants, Hirkalla has moved an army up to the city limits.

Rumours of a certain creature, the "Madman", living in the catacombs beneath the city, are also cause for alarm. It is said that this creature was resurrected by Hirkalla herself and is her secret weapon, meant to keep the Great Mages in fear. The Council is aware of certain speculations regarding the identity of this creature. But I would note that there are no grounds whatsoever for such rumours. Let me stress this in particular: no grounds whatsoever!

In order to minimise risks, Hirkalla is fencing off parts of the allod with magical barriers, restricting access to them. Therefore the area open to visitors to the allod is the city of Dar Ulgorn itself and its immediate surroundings.

Apart from the events on the allod of Jigran, an era of relative calm has now set in across the rest of Sarnaut. The conflicts have ceased; everyone fears Hirkalla and her army of the dead.

With this I conclude my report on what I have been able to learn on the allod of Jigran during the past period. I shall keep watching the situation and will inform the Council of all new events in due course.
