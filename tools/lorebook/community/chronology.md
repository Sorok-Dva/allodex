## Before the Old Era

- **(millions of years before the present events)** The Battle of the Gods takes place. Nihaz, the God of Darkness, imprisons Sarn, the God of Light, in the Prison World of Sarnaut. The Dragons become Sarn's jailers, among them the twelve Great Guardian Dragons.

- **(later)** A meteorite falls on Sarnaut, killing three of the Guardians. Sarn gains the ability to reshape the planet. Over millions of years he carries out his plan: to create intelligent life whose representatives will be able to destroy the Dragons. The Guardians notice Sarn's attempts and strive to wipe out every form of life.

## The Old Era

- **(~1)** Sarnaut, the Old Era: the beginning of the official reckoning of years according to the chronicles of the elves. Many intelligent races created by Sarn inhabit the planet. The largest continent is Yul; the best-known large islands are Isa and Irdrich.

- **(350–1700)** The Battle for Beauty: the first elves destroy everything that does not fit their idea of Beauty. During this war they meet the first human tribe, the June, and teach them magic. The elven state itself is called Irene.

- **(1700–2100)** The Transformation of the Elves. Having met the Dragons, the elves conclude that the Dragons destroy other races because of their own idea of beauty. They decide to change themselves and become like the dragons. Meanwhile the June reach an unprecedented flowering and successfully resist the dragons' attempts to destroy them.

- **(1800)** The June colonise Irdrich and take the local race, the Tekyan, under their protection.

- **(1900)** Preparations for the Transformation of the elves are complete, and they enter a magical slumber for 200 years.

- **(shortly before 2000)** Sarn, their creator, appears to the June. The June swear allegiance to him, and he blesses them to destroy the remaining Guardians. The June succeed in killing eight of the remaining Great Dragons. The Dragons send a messenger to Nihaz asking for help.

- **(2000)** In answer to the Dragons' pleas, Nihaz appears to the June. He tells them that if they kill the last of the Guardians, Sarnaut will be destroyed. In exchange for betraying Sarn, Nihaz promises the June "a new world". When the June betray their Creator, Sarn curses them, and his Curse takes material form: a demon. The Curse destroys the civilisation of the June. The remnants of this people turn to Nihaz in supplication, reminding him of his promise, and he transforms them into Wormfaces, loathsome creatures living underground. Thus these June receive their "new world", but they cease to be June, and the Curse no longer pursues them. Some of the June, however, manage to hide, despite the Curse that follows them. After these events Nihaz becomes disillusioned with the Dragons and rebuilds the defences of the prison world in the form of magical Seals.

- **(2050–2500)** The attempts of the Zem, a human people from the southern land of Hickut, to find the source of immortality. Frightened by the fate of the June, the Zem seek salvation from death. Beginning with experiments in necromancy, they move on to prosthetics and mechanisation.

- **(2100)** The new elves. The Transformation does not turn out as originally planned, but the elves decide not to begin a new transformation and to leave things as they are: after all, both the dragons and the June have almost all perished, so Beauty is not an absolute ideal. From now on the ideal of the elves is Pleasure.

- **(2298)** The Conclave of Great Mages is formed, at first made up of elves only.

- **(around 2500)** Tep, a necromancer of the Zem people, uses a shard of one of Nihaz's Seals to create a magical plague meant to slaughter his own people. His aim is to fill the Spark Repositories quickly: the Pyramids, which grant him immortality. The plague wipes out almost the entire Zem people at once, but the last group of survivors, the Mechanics, manage to find the necromancer's refuge. They all die in the battle, but they destroy Tep's protection against his own plague. As a result Tep, locked in the pyramid, dies and is resurrected again and again for thousands of years.

- **(2500–2600)** The human tribes of the Aro and the orcish Horde clash on the ruins of the June civilisation, and war begins.

- **(2570)** The scattered Aro tribes are united into a single state, Kania. Humans join the Conclave of Great Mages: first Skrakan, then his disciples Tensess and Nezeb. After the mysterious death of the orc chieftain Skulnar, the Horde breaks up into warring clans, and Kania wins. Soon after the end of the war with the orcs, a rivalry for power arises between Tensess and Nezeb. After a magical duel, the defeated Nezeb is exiled to the southern deserts, to the ruins of the Zem civilisation, where he meets the nomadic Ugra tribes and becomes their leader. The Conclave of Great Mages splits.

- **(2743)** The elf Zayan, Great Mage and historian, completes the "Chronicles of the Past".

- **(2910)** The Ugra tribes, now called the Xadaganians, invade the southern provinces of Kania.

- **(2911)** The Battle of Molod between the Kanians and the Xadaganians. Tensess offers a truce, leaving Nezeb the territories he has conquered. After this, power changes hands in Kania itself, and the Valir dynasty comes to rule it. Wars between Kania and Xadagan flare up again and again over thousands of years.

- **(around 3400)** Skrakan succeeds in reuniting the Conclave of Great Mages.

- **(4015)** The Grand Experiment of the Conclave of Great Mages. Seeking even greater power, the Great Mages find the Seals of Nihaz. Trying to bend them to their will, they break the Seals.

## The New Era – the Era of Allods

- **(1)** The New Era, the Cataclysm. When the Seals cracked, a primordial force poured out into the world: the Astral. At once it began to devour the solid land, destroying everything. But the Great Mages still manage to halt the advance of the Astral by creating a special ritual of Stasis (the key to which is Dragon Blood). And now each Great Mage becomes the eternal prisoner of the lands he holds. These surviving islands of land are called Allods, and so begins a new Era, the Era of Allods. Travel between allods by portal is far from easy, and so Sarnaut becomes divided. Nezeb begins to build a cult of himself among the Xadaganians, mixing a narcotic salt into the drinking springs and thereby making his subjects more suggestible. Among the elves, all the politics once conducted at the Great Ball gradually falls under the power of the mightiest Eight Houses. Kania is once again led by the Great Mage Tensess. The Valir dynasty now rules only a shard of its Empire: Kvatoh. Semi-independent monarchies of their own also arise on many other allods. And many civilisations disappear altogether or degenerate.

- **(some time after the Cataclysm)** Sarn, now utterly mad, creates the race of demons: perfect destroyers who dwell in the Astral.

- **(356)** The Conclave splits into a Kanian part and a Xadaganian part.

- **(546)** The first expedition to Gipat, undertaken by the Xadaganians.

- **(576)** Skrakan summons the Demon. Skrakan manages to seal his allod, Umoir, with a special spell that lets nothing leave the allod, before the Demon turns him to stone. Under the Demon's influence the flora and fauna of Umoir begin to mutate into hideous creatures.

- **(646)** Four envoys of the Kanian emperor Valir IV and of Tensess, Danas, Naira, Reniesta and Fergard, set out for Umoir. They succeed in destroying the Demon and freeing Skrakan (the story of Allods: Seal of Mystery).

- **(676)** Skrakan notices that strange sorcery, similar to the Demon's magic, is at work on the Xadaganian allod of Yazes. He sends his envoy, who manages to untangle a web of intrigue that leads to the Great Mage of Yazes, the Necromancer Urd. One of the envoy's companions, the knight Iglez, becomes the new king (the story of Allods II: Master of Souls).

- **(702)** The orc Rodogor, who had earlier tried without success to learn magic from the elf Claude de Veuvre, becomes supreme shaman and unites the orcs. He then makes an alliance with Nezeb.

- **(707)** The forces of Xadagan and the orc tribes capture Grokh, killing the elven Great Mage Claude de Veuvre. House de Veuvre calls an extraordinary Great Ball and concludes an alliance with Kania. Thus the League is created.

- **(725)** The Battle for the allod of Kech. The Empire captures this Kanian allod.

- **(741)** The first known resurrection of the Arisen Zem takes place on Igsh.

- **(765)** The first astral ship of the Gibberlings appears near the domains of House de Grandeur.

- **(soon after 765)** The Gibberlings land on the allod of Izune; the orcs try to enslave them, and the Gibberlings flee from Izune to Kvatoh.

- **(786)** Xadagan sends an expedition to Gipat to search for a fallen meteorite. At the same time a young June named Ath-Zak, drawing the Curse away from his beloved, flees to Suslanger, changes his appearance and returns to Gipat, having lost his memory. In the village he is taken for the Chosen One. Meanwhile on Gipat Zak, called the Chosen One, helps the locals, making the village safe from the threat of goblins and orcs. Then, trying to recover his memory, he meets Tka-Rik, the Great Mage of Gipat. Not knowing that Zak is the transformed June Ath-Zak, Tka-Rik asks him to bring Ath-Zak's beloved, Lua-Jalla, from Lightwood. Zak falls in love with her and brings her to the mage on Gipat; then Tka-Rik sends Zak to Suslanger to find the June youth. Zak returns empty-handed. Tka-Rik studies a fragment of a teleportation amulet and restores Zak to his former appearance of Ath-Zak. Together, Ath-Zak and Tka-Rik defeat the Curse of the June (the story of "Evil Islands").

- **(786)** (non-canon) At this time on Suslanger the youth Kir, having escaped captivity under the mage Firez on the allod of Jigran, meets Kel. Then they arrive on Gipat. They help the orcs subdue the humans, break everything the Chosen One had built and set off for Lightwood, where they find a portal to Jigran. Back on Jigran, they actively help Kania in its battles against Xadagan, embodied by the troops of Firez, and win. Firez kills Kel; Kir kills Firez ("Evil Islands: Lost in Astral").

- **(800)** The Gibberlings join the League, handing over the secret of astral ships in exchange for the lands of the allod of Lightwood. House de Grandeur, which took part in concluding the alliance, wins the Great Game. The Gibberlings' secret is meteoric iron, which resists the effect of the Astral and protects nearby objects as well (the story of the book "All the Ships of the Astral").

- **(805)** The first revolt of the Arisen Zem on Suslanger, in protest against slavery.

- **(821)** The resurrection of Nefer Ur, a necromancer and mechanic of the Zem tribe who died in the struggle against Tep. Nefer Ur is one of the few who know the reasons that led to the death of his people. Having analysed the current state of affairs, he works out his plan and raises a rebellion.

- **(823)** The elven House de Ardeur wins the Great Game, having created the prototype of the League's first astral ship based on the Gibberlings' secret.

- **(825)** Nezeb meets Nefer Ur; Nezeb signs a decree forbidding the use of resurrected Zem as slaves.

- **(850)** The Arisen, though not full citizens of the Empire, nonetheless make a considerable contribution to scientific and technical progress, and the life of the Xadaganians changes fundamentally. The ZIT Institute (Magic and Legacy of the Zem People) is founded, headed by Nefer Ur.

- **(864)** At the Great Ball the elves adopt a new strategic doctrine of cultural expansion towards other races.

- **(901)** The elven House de Doucer wins the Great Game. They become the instigators of an attack on the allod of Kirah, which holds large deposits of meteoric iron (the Empire mines it as a material for armour and weapons, unaware of all its properties). Moreover, Kirah holds the grave of one of the Great Dragons and its still-bleeding heart (and Dragon blood is what the Great Mages need to hold an Allod). Preparations proceed in complete secrecy.

- **(907)** The Arisen are recognised as citizens of the Empire, and the allod of Xadagan is handed over to the Arisen. Nefer Ur becomes the holder of this allod.

- **(909)** League troops land on the allod of Kirah, led by Eniel de Ardeur. In response the Empire sends Rodogor's Regiment of Kirah, led by the young Great Mage Yasker. War begins.

- **(4 March 910)** On the night before the decisive battle on Kirah, the event known as the "Night of Astral Portals" takes place. Quite unexpectedly, portals open on every allod next to the Towers of the Great Mages, and armadas of demons burst out of them. This sudden blow has catastrophic consequences and costs the lives of millions of living beings, for after a Great Mage dies the Astral begins to devour his allod. Among the known events: the destruction of the allods of Gipat, Kirah, Yazes and Scarge, the destruction of the capitals on the allods of Umoir and Kvatoh, the death of the Great Mage Tensess, and the flight of the dragoness Soliskar from Izune. More than half of the allods were lost, and those that held out shrank considerably in size.

- **(4 March 910, alternate timeline)** Visitors from the future change the past; as a result, the allod of Kirah is not destroyed.

- **(910)** The demon invasion is repelled at a heavy price. The first units of League Defenders and Imperial Sentinels are formed: special divisions for the war against demons. In addition, the neutral organisation of Demon Hunters is created. Striving for maximum effectiveness in the fight against the enemy, the Hunters undertake risky experiments that alter their nature. The demon invasions continue, though no longer on such a massive scale. Skrakan becomes the holder of the Allod of Kvatoh; his place on Umoir is taken by Catherina.

- **(911)** The Great Mage Tensess died during the Night of Astral Portals. His Spark was captured by one of Tep's pyramids. When Tensess found himself in the Pyramid, someone spoke to him and said that they were granting him and the world a new power: the power of Light, whose source would be faith. In the end, using his magical power and the capabilities of Tep's Pyramid, Tensess was able to grant the inhabitants of Sarnaut the gift of resurrection. To take control of the power of Tep's Pyramid, the Great Mage of Kania had to fight Tep himself and drive him out of the Pyramid. However, the banished Tep laid a special curse on the magical artefacts of this world. Soon afterwards one of the League's ships is wrecked near a hitherto unknown Allod, and its crew perishes. Some time later they are resurrected on this allod and find the pyramid where the spirit of Tensess is imprisoned. From then on this Allod becomes known as the Holy Land. Believers in the Light begin to venerate Tensess as a Saint and create the Church of Light.

- **(915)** A period of calm sets in in the war with the demons. The raids cease, and the coastal Astral becomes free and safe again.

- **(930)** The second major demon invasion, this time repelled without great losses.

- **(945)** Through the treachery of the goblins whom the Gibberlings had evacuated from the allod of Izune, the Empire captures an astral ship and learns the secret of the meteorite. Soon the Zem uncover the secret of astral power. Construction begins on a huge fleet of ships of a new type.

- **(957)** A new war between the League and the Empire begins. The Imperial fleet crushes the League's fleet and blockades its allods, securing the landing of troops. On the allods themselves, however, the war soon becomes drawn out, and no quick success can be achieved. In one of the battles Yasker defeats Eniel, whom he had fought earlier on Kirah (the story of the book "The Great Game").

- **(960)** The appearance of the first demon cultists, who welcome the coming of the demons, regarding them either as a deserved punishment for the inhabitants of the allods, or as saviours, or even as envoys of the forces of evil whom it is worth serving and helping in order to escape inevitable doom.

- **(962)** The young Kanian paladin Voisvet Belov succeeds in capturing a Xadaganian ship. Elven scholars uncover the secret of astral power. Soon the League begins to build ships like those of the Empire, and then lifts the blockade of its allods.

- **(965)** A League ship patrolling the Astral near the shards of Gipat finds a small, previously unknown allod. The ship's captain goes down into its underground halls and some time later returns holding the "Revelations of Tka-Rik". In this document Tka-Rik reported that the demons had been summoned by the June from another world and that there was a gate whose closing could prevent further invasions. Skrakan makes these documents public, and the war between the League and the Empire stops. Everyone blames the June, but no one knows that the document is in fact a forgery created by Sarn in order to lure the Great Mages into a trap.

- **(966)** The Great Astral Campaign. Skrakan and Nezeb meet and sign a peace treaty between the League and the Empire in order to create a joint fleet and close the Gate. All the forces of the League and the Empire are thrown into this battle. Skrakan and Nezeb themselves lead the fleet, leaving the capital allods in the care of Aidenus and Yasker. Yet only a few are lucky enough to survive the astral storm that arose after the "Gate of the June" was sealed. It was believed that at the cost of their own lives Skrakan, Nezeb and a great many ordinary warriors had saved the world of the allods. This became one of the dogmas of the League's Church of Light, while in the Empire the Trinity Church was created, which held that the sacrifice of Skrakan and Nezeb was equal to the sacrifice of Tensess.

- **(967)** Civil war breaks out in the Empire between the Great Mages Yasker and Gorluxor, who both claim leadership of the Empire. Yasker wins by promising full equality to the orcs and the Arisen. In the battle for Igsh, Gorluxor is defeated and flees.

- **(972)** (non-canon) An attempt by Tep's cultists to break into the Pyramid on the Holy Land and find their master (the story of the book "A Game in the Halls of Death").

- **(981)** Elizaveta Rysina takes the post of Head of the Nezeb Committee.

- **(990)** At another Great Ball, House de Desirae wins thanks to a victorious duel in the art of necromancy between Prince Dakkar de Desirae and the head of House de Doucer, Armando. Soon afterwards Prince Dakkar's bride, a human girl named Svetlana from the princely Valir family, suddenly dies. The prince's attempt to bring her back to life fails, a curse falls on the Valir castle, and its inhabitants turn into undead. Prince Dakkar goes into voluntary exile. The investigation reveals that the defeated Armando was involved in what happened. House de Doucer is declared outlaw for breaking the rules of the Great Game. As it later turned out, Armando had enlisted the help of Tep, who taught him the ancient magic of blood. After this, Armando and his closest followers become vampires.

- **(1005)** Elections of Viceroys in the League. Voisvet Belov (who changed his surname to Zhelezny) becomes Viceroy of the allod of Kvatoh, Ivan the Ascetic becomes Viceroy of Forox, and Catherina Viceroy of Umoir. The old aristocracy, having lost power, goes into opposition. On Umoir, Catherina begins to persecute the nobles, the pagans and other races, driving the minor elven House de Tristesse out of Umoir.

- **(1008)** Accumulated tensions lead to a war between the Empire and the League over the Holy Land.

- **(1010)** Several conspiracies organised by Tep on the capital allods of the League and the Empire are uncovered. A new war with the demons: the leaders of the League and the Empire realise that the Great Astral Campaign did not eliminate the threat. Having concluded a truce, they defeat Gorluxor in the City of Demons. A new Revelation on Gipat, where it turns out that the Wormfaces and the June are one and the same, and that the earlier Revelation of Tka-Rik was a cultists' forgery. A meeting with Nihaz, a journey into the past to Kirah, and the prevention of its destruction (the story of levels 1 to 45).

- **(1011)** Nihaz sends the heroes of the League and the Empire in search of the true Revelation. An alliance with the Draconids and the Wormfaces (the story of levels 45 to 47).

- **(1012)** The Draconids and the Wormfaces, having got what they wanted from the alliance, break it off. After obtaining the true Revelation about the nature of the Game of the Gods, the heroes learn from Nihaz that the goal of the Draconids, the Wormfaces and Sarn is the Gate to other worlds, located in the Dead City on one of the shards of Gipat. After certain difficulties and a temporary alliance with the cultists, the heroes, with Nihaz's help, create a new allod on which stands a portal to the Dead City. At last, in the Dead City, the heroes defeat the leaders of the Draconids, the Wormfaces and the demons, but then, realising that Nihaz is dangerous to them as well, they destroy his avatar too. Nihaz tells of his Seals and reveals that it was he who gave the first impulse to the faith in the Light, back then in the Pyramid on the Holy Land (the story of levels 47 to 51, season 3, "Game of Gods").

- **(1013)** A clash with Tep, who has lured the fleets of the League and the Empire into a trap. With the help of Nihaz's "daughter", Klavdia, the heroes wreck Tep's plans and then defeat Tep himself. Klavdia seizes his spark and disappears. The Sparks of Skrakan and Nezeb tell the heroes the truth about the Grand Experiment and the causes of the Cataclysm (the story of levels 51 to 55, season 4, "Lords of Destiny").

- **(1014)** Yasker and Aidenus send the heroes in pursuit of Klavdia. The chase leads them to the allod of Umoir, which has been struck by a magical disease, the Blight. In the end they learn that the Blight was sent by Sarn and that the Great Mage Catherina is possessed by him. When Catherina sets out for the Heart of the World in order to destroy the Seals once and for all, she is freed from the possession. Along the way Klavdia's motives also come to light: she is expecting some great trouble in the future and is gathering strength for it (the story of levels 55 to 60, season 5, "Heart of the World").

- **(1015)** Zayan, head of the historians, learns that Tep once visited the mysterious allod of Ferris and sends an expedition there to find out whether there are any dangerous artefacts and what happened there at all. The expedition finds an old abandoned Imperial research laboratory: the "Gate" project. There they also find and free a representative of an otherworldly race of Architects possessing a single shared consciousness: the Sculptor. The expedition and the forces of the League and the Empire that arrive manage to close the portal leading to the world of the Architects, but the Sculptor escapes (the story of levels 60 to 65, season 6, "Broken Chains").

- **(1016)** The Architects invade the allod of Dane. With enormous effort they are beaten back and yet another portal is closed. After this, the "Order" cult is discovered on Dane, in whose creation the Sculptor had a hand. The cult is destroyed. Soon afterwards Klavdia Kalugina carries out an insane plan: to open the Star Gate, letting the Architects into Sarnaut, and at the same time to free Sarn. In a monstrous battle Sarn and the Architect destroy each other (the story of levels 66 to 70, season 7, "New Order").

- **(1017)** From the divine energy that poured into the Astral after the death of the two Gods, the allod of Ammer arises, together with the exalted race of the Aoidoi, who combine qualities of both Sarn and the Architect. In the first months of Ammer's existence, time on it flows noticeably faster than in the rest of Sarnaut: while only a year passes outside, several centuries go by on this allod, and the Aoidoi build a civilisation. Later time stabilises and the astral storm subsides, allowing explorers of the League and the Empire to land on the island and make contact with the Aoidoi. A clash takes place with the mad Aoidos Kyros, who seeks to kill the Servant of Tensess and deprive the world of the Gift of Resurrection. Soon afterwards the Well of Souls, the source of life of all the Aoidoi, gives birth to Cosmos, the Dominus of Creation. At the height of the Dionic Games he organises, Nihaz makes himself known again, threatening to plunge all of Sarnaut into Darkness. With the help of the Observatory created by Cosmos, the heroes travel into the future, where they see the world of the Allods almost destroyed by Nihaz, and into the past, where they free the orc psionicist Skulnar the Fierce, a possible descendant of the legendary orcish Great Mage, from Imperial captivity (the story of levels 71 to 75, season 8, "Immortality").

- **(1018)** An expedition of the Aoidoi discovers the allod of Sogot in the distant Astral, where members of the Demonologists organisation have allied themselves with demons freed from Sarn's power after his death. The allod comes under attack by annihilator demons controlled by Nihaz, during which the main hero's mind falls under the influence of the Dark God. To be healed, the hero asks for help from the Sculptor, rescued from the future, and from Klavdia Kalugina, who helps him understand the nature of Nihaz better (the story of levels 76 to 80, season 9, "Awakening").

- **(1019)** Seers all over Sarnaut receive disturbing visions of the terrible future that awaits the Allods: one day the Darkness of Nihaz will swallow everything, and only a handful of people will survive, saved on arks and finding refuge on the allod of Horizon. The seeress Unn gives the hero the chance to live in the skin of one of the distant descendants of the survivors, but she also hints that there is a way to avoid such a future (the story of levels 81 to 85, season 10, "Destination Horizon").

- **(1020)** Open war begins between the races of Sarnaut and the returned Nihaz. Klavdia Kalugina and the best necromancers of the Allods gather on Umoir an army of undead capable of giving battle to the host of the Dark God. The united army besieges Nihaz's Citadel itself, from which he controls the Seals that bind the world. Meanwhile the elves discover a shard of the ancient kingdom of Irene and decide to hold another Great Ball on it. During the Ball it turns out that Skulnar the Fierce is indeed a potential Great Mage, which causes unrest among the Imperial orcs (the story of levels 86 to 90, season 11, "Soul of Darkness").

- **(1021)** On the allod of Suslanger, construction begins on a grand joint project of the League and the Empire: yet another Gate to another world. During an unknown incident, an unknown substance, named ambrosia, is thrown out of the Gate into Sarnaut; it can cause mutations in living beings. Meanwhile a schism is brewing in the Empire: more and more orcs speak out for independence, predicting the imminent rise of the Legendary Orc (the story of levels 91 to 95, season 12, "Desert Dreams").
