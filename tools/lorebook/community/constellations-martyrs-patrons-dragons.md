- Arg – Cancer (Lat. Cancer) – Tegmine – Brilliant as silver – Water
- Ulf – Aries (Lat. Aries) – Sheratan, the Fire Father – Hot as sulphur – Fire
- Lex – Taurus (Lat. Taurus) – Elnath – Sharp as flint – Earth
- Hyrd – Gemini (Lat. Gemini) – Alzirr, Father of Winds, the Northern Dragon, Nordrak – Swift as mercury – Air

- Irsen – Virgo (Lat. Virgo) – Syrma – Caustic as arsenic – Order
- Caprey – Libra (Lat. Libra) – Zuben – Strong as copper – Darkness
- Ferr – Scorpio (Lat. Scorpius) – Sargas – Reliable as iron – Chaos
- Uar – Leo (Lat. Leo) – Regulus – Deadly as gold – Light

- Stagan – Sagittarius (Lat. Sagittarius) – Rukbat – Steadfast as tin – Destruction
- Hos – Aquarius (Lat. Aquarius) – Situla – Ardent as phosphorus – Creation
- Rokoit – Pisces (Lat. Pisces) – Revati – Hard as chromium – Life
- Plam – Capricorn (Lat. Capricornus) – Nashira – Heavy as lead – Death
