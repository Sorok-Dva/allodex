## Irene

Oh, that famous story, directly bound up with Irene! Once, the last kingdom of the elves stood here. During the Cataclysm it survived thanks to an unusual artifact kept by King Grallon – the Key of Creation. And the king had a daughter, the young Princess Daguta, who could never find herself a suitor…

All was well until a mysterious Stranger, guided by aims and designs of his own, appeared at the castle gates! He seduced Daguta, forced her to steal the Key, and so doomed everyone!

King Grallon managed to escape with a handful of courtiers, and they watched Irene sink into the depths of the Astral. But, as we now know, the allod was not destroyed completely after all…

Once the elves had a single kingdom – Irene. After the fall of the June nothing could compare with the might of this kingdom, in which even the lowliest vagabond wielded magic. Striving to become stronger still, the elite of this elven kingdom delved ever deeper into realms of magic unexplored until then.

The attention of the elves was drawn to the mysterious magic of the June, blood magic. Through the rituals of blood magic, some elves became vampires and were doomed to an eternal thirst that could only be quenched with the blood of others. These events came to be known as the "Blood Feast".

The problem that arose was eventually brought to naught, but it did not pass without a trace for Irene: trust among the elves had been destroyed. Many of them left the bounds of the kingdom and settled in various hidden corners of Yul. A little later the elves began to form Houses, family clans, each of which lived by its own laws and rules.

## Ammer

An allod born after the death of two gods. Both fell in a cruel battle. The energy released at the moment of their death was so great that it pierced the whole world and took shape as the Well of Souls. The Astral hastily retreated from it, allowing a new allod to grow out of nothing. A few days later the first Aoidoi were born. The plants and animals here possess colossal vitality and develop in an utterly chaotic way.

## Ark

There was one place in Sarnaut where the enmity between Kania and Xadagan did not matter. A remarkable place it was – the allod of Ark, ruled since olden times by the Great Mage Erast. The valley in which Erast's domain lay is an extraordinarily beautiful spot. Small rivers flowed down from the mountains, and everything around was planted with gardens full of roses.

Ark was originally a small principality in the far south-west of Kania. Nezeb's conquest, when he unleashed the hordes of Ugra upon the southern borders, passed it by, but at the same time cut it off from the main body of the Kanian lands.

Erast himself considered himself independent, but he did pay Nezeb various marks of respect – he was, after all, his nearest neighbour. The Xadaganian emperor, for his part, did not dare to touch Ark either: the profits of trade were considerable, and there would be no benefit in simply seizing such a scrap of land.

If one were to choose a place to live as one pleased, Ark might well have been it. Neither Kania nor Xadagan collected taxes or recruited troops there: live and let live. All the more so as there were hardly any threats on Ark… Except perhaps for a few trolls and kobolds in the mountain caves, but they did not risk coming down into the valley. And the people of Ark were, of course, a cheerful lot. Kanians and Xadaganians had long since mingled here, and one could no longer tell them apart. Elves also lived in these lost lands – the House de Ark.

## Gelida

A rather… harsh place, an island of eternal winter, although the local forests are full of all kinds of beasts, and the rivers and lakes are full of fish.

## Grok

A vast steppe; small forests and mountains are found here only at the edges of the island.

Originally the allod belonged to the elves of the House de Vevre, who had several small towns here. Admittedly, the local steppes are not very fertile – there are few rivers, cold winds often blew, so grain did not grow very well either. Magic helped the elves with this, and besides magic they found another solution: breeding all manner of livestock.

But there was one misfortune on Grok that constantly disturbed the peaceful life of the elves. And the name of that misfortune was orcs. There were countless orcs here, and they lived in the steppes, a little further from the centre of the allod. The House de Vevre, of course, would have liked to be rid of the orcs – but how do you chase them across the steppes?

Everything changed in the year 707 of the New Era, when the orcs and the Xadaganians captured Grok, killing Claude de Vevre, the Great Mage of the allod. Nezeb kept the promise he had given Rodogor, and the allod came to be held by a Xadaganian Great Mage.

An expedition of historians sent out (recently) managed to find part of the documents from the archive of the Great Mage Mark, which had survived on one of the shards of the destroyed allod.

As it turned out, the Empire had found elven documents describing events connected with the Great Orc. In the days when the allod was still held by an elven Great Mage, an unusual child was born into the tribe of orcs that lived on Grok – Lorzub...

The Xadaganians who found the records of the elven Great Mage Claude de Vevre all the more preferred to hide from everyone the fact that such an orc had really existed, not wishing to give the shamans hope of finally breaking away from the Empire. But now the legacy of the Legendary Orc has returned to the world – who knows what it will lead to?

One of the legends passed in a whisper from mothers to children is the tale of an orc who wielded magic. Time has erased the details of this story – no one can even say for certain on which of the allods it happened, or when. But the legend lives its own life – and gives hope to the shamans, who have long wished to throw off the yoke of the Empire's Great Mages and declare the allod of Izune free.

The Xadaganians themselves consider this claim entirely unconfirmed. In their view, the magical abilities that occasionally appear among orcs of the Fierce clan are the result of astral mutation. Additional data could have come from a study of the allod from which Skulnar's ancestors hail – Grok – but that allod is now lost, torn apart by astral demons during their first deadly attack.

It remains only to wonder: what if the Legendary Orc is no accident, no blind play of fate, but someone's creation? Who possesses a power capable of transforming a living being in such a way? And for what purpose was this deed done?

## Lightwood

Wintry. Coniferous forests. Representatives of the House de Craner have now opened a fencing school on the allod of Lightwood, where you can meet elves as well as Kanians and Gibberlings!

## Yoggor

Yoggor – once the domain of the House de Doucer…

The Great Mage of this allod was the wise Gramont de Doucer, a pupil of the elven mages of antiquity, only slightly inferior in power to Skrakan. He gathered everyone who found themselves on his allod after the Night of Woe into one single great House and revived the tradition of the Great Houses, in which the remaining Eight Great Houses now began to take part.

Yoggor itself is a rather unusual place, a majestic forest full of enormous trees. Most of the elves live in their own castles, scattered in great numbers across the allod. Although only elves inhabit this allod, there are also those here capable of posing a threat to lone travellers – various mindless creatures such as giant spiders and mantises. But everything comes to an end, and so a terrible fate befell Yoggor as well – an extraordinarily deadly spell that changed the usual way of life on the allod, shrouding it in darkness.

## Xadagan

The lands that now lie within the allod of Xadagan are considered the centre of the Zem civilisation. Before the sands swallowed the ancient ruins, the state of Hickut existed here, in the river valley. When the Zem were wiped out by the plague, the old irrigation systems were destroyed, and only a few oases gave shelter to the peoples of Ugra, who roamed the desert and came to replace the builders of the pyramids.

But then the Cataclysm destroyed the pyramids, which had been traps for Sparks, and the Arisen returned, quite literally, from the other world. Over the millennia the world had changed a great deal and was far from friendly towards them – at first all that awaited them was either death or slavery in the mines.

The Zem had to work hard to win back their lands – in the end a compromise was found. Nefer Ur, formerly the Chief Mechanic of the Zem people, became the Great Mage, the keeper of the allod of Xadagan.

Much has changed on this allod since then: the Zem have transformed it according to their own tastes and needs. Old buildings dug out of the sand stand side by side with brand-new structures sparkling with the lights of modern technology. To many Xadaganians this look seems too mechanical and lifeless, but for the rational Zem it is just what is needed. Today the Arisen are ready to act in alliance with the other races of the Empire.

## Malar

A very peaceful allod, inhabited since the most ancient times, when Kania was a single state rather than a collection of separate islands. Usually nothing happens here; even the demons hardly ever attacked. There are no large cities, but a great many small villages and farmsteads. Vast forests, in which a stranger can very easily lose his way.

## Ney

Ney is not a very large allod, although it was here that Zayan, together with Skrakan, first succeeded in creating the Stasis ritual. It lies in the middle of a plain that was once high in the mountains. The local landmark deserves a mention too – Rakhl-Ney, a tower that is almost the only building on the allod. Today the Council of Historians sits in it. The tower itself is a grandiose library, with shelves the size of several houses.

Of course, it is not only books that are kept in Rakhl-Ney: there are plenty of different artifacts here as well.

Besides the tower itself, the sights here include an old system of portals leading to a great many allods. This was something like a crossroads where many of the magical paths of the June tribe converged… It is only a pity that many of the portals no longer work.

## Stellara

The allod is a serene forest beneath a starry night sky. It is also home to the principal school of magic for elves, where they are sent while still small children. Apart from the elves, the only inhabitants are lamias, whom we teach magic. From them a special "magical support regiment" has now been formed, commanded by a certain mysterious lady.

## Tempesta

A harsh place where a thunderstorm rages constantly, an eternal season of tempests. Our buildings stand on the shore of a huge lake, on sheer cliffs, but we do not complain of the inconvenience, for we have managed to secure ample comfort inside our homes, protected from any bad weather. The local tribe of minotaurs has become our faithful servants and has proved itself excellent at guarding our goods.

## Florenta

A blossoming place where, it seems, there are no seasons but spring and summer. Yes, the whole allod is covered in flowers and shrubs, the scent makes your head spin, and the buzzing of countless bees drowns out every other sound. Harpies also live on the allod, and they have long served the House de Vevre.

## Yakhch

Yakhch was once an ordinary desert allod, but it was here, in their day, that the druids came, having decided to try planting a seed of the Great Tree in one of the oases.

The Tree changed the surrounding landscape of Yakhch enormously. Where there was once desert, mighty forests now rise – a true taiga. The climate changed as well: there is more rainfall, and on the whole it is colder. Now this land is a source of timber, which the Xadaganians ship to various regions of their state.

None of the Great Mages knows what the Great Trees really are. It is believed that the first of them grew somewhere on a northern island, and appeared on Yul noticeably later, when some merchant managed to obtain their seeds. However, in the Era of Allods it turned out that even a small sapling of the Tree can protect a whole city from the Astral.
